# KIOSK Platform — Project Brief

## Product
KIOSK is an all-in-one business operating platform for managing sales, customers, inventory, staff, purchasing, reporting, alerts, and configurable business workflows from one system.

Tagline: **Your business, under one roof.**

## Product strategy
Build from a clean foundation and selectively adopt the strongest concepts, workflows, and modules from previous projects. Do not merge legacy codebases blindly.

## Primary users
- Business owner
- Admin
- Manager
- Cashier / sales staff
- Inventory / purchasing staff
- General employee

## Core modules
- Dashboard
- Point of Sale
- Customers / CRM
- Inventory
- Pricing
- Purchasing / receiving
- Employees
- Roles and permissions
- Time clock / attendance
- Sales history
- Financial and operational reports
- End-of-day close
- Alerts and reminders
- Settings / customization

## Product principles
- Modular
- Multi-business friendly
- Role-aware
- Fast at checkout
- Audit-friendly
- Responsive
- Easy to customize without rebuilding the product

## Reference repositories
Use `sync-smart-crm`, `kioskcrm`, and `weave-lane` as reference sources for selected concepts and modules only.

## Initial success criteria
- Clear reusable shell/navigation
- Working authentication and role model
- Stable product/inventory/customer foundations
- Reliable POS flow
- Accurate transaction history
- End-of-day reporting
- Production-ready Vercel deployment
